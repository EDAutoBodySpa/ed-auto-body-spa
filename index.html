02 — DECON

/ About Us

Locals who care about cars

Erik and Dennis started E&D Auto Body Spa in 2025 because they were tired of watching good cars get treated poorly at big chain shops. We're Baltimore County folks — neighbors, not strangers.

Same guy handles every detail, from start to finish. No rotating crews, no rushing through jobs. Just honest work and a finish you'll notice.
